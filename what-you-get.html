Course layer 4 workbooks

  • L1 — Deliverability Fundamentals. Six lessons, in order: why most cold email failures are infrastructure and not copy; setting up SPF, DKIM and DMARC in the right order; building a 30-address seed list you actually trust (twenty is the floor, forty the ceiling); running an inbox placement test and reading the result; when a domain is burnt and how to stop the bleed; writing the client-facing note when placement drops.
  • L2 — The Sequence and Copy Swipe File. Five lessons: the four sequence shapes and when each one wins; the subject lines, how the 80 were selected and how to write your own; objection handling, the 24 replies and what to send back; re-engaging a cold list without burning it; referral intros.
  • L3 — List, ICP and Infrastructure QA. Six lessons: writing an ICP that produces a buying list, not a persona document; scoring offer-market fit before you buy a single address; the 14-check list purchase QA and why 3 percent is a walk-away; verifying addresses and the eight classes; the release gate and its twelve sign-offs; running a validation interview that tells you the truth.
  • L4 — Retention Runbook and Team Layer. Seven lessons: the weekly pulse clients actually open; scoring client health before they tell you they are leaving; the churn scorecard and the save conversation; the quarterly business review, turning a report into a renewal; renewal and price increase scripts, and when not to raise; the 90-day runbook and the SOP that holds it; pipeline math.

Every lesson opens with one outcome, teaches the mechanism, ends with an action to run now, and asks one question before you move on. Level 1 puts a clock on each action, fifteen to twenty minutes. Levels 2, 3 and 4 state the action without a time budget, and one Level 3 lesson asks you to book three interviews across a week rather than sit down for an hour. Where no time is given, read the action as one sitting and stop when the output exists.
