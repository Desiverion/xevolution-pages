Privacy Policy

The Outbound Agency Vault · Xevolution · last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information we hold, why we hold it, and how to make us stop.

What we collect

  • What you give at checkout: your email address, name, billing address and phone number where provided.
  • What the payment processor sees: your card details. These are handled entirely by Stripe. They never reach us and we cannot see them.
  • What we keep about your order: what you bought, the date, the amount, and the delivery correspondence.

Why we hold it

  • To deliver what you bought and to resend it if you ask.
  • To handle refunds and answer support mail.
  • To keep accounting records we are required to keep.
  • To tell you about a material change to a product you have bought - not general marketing, unless you asked for it.

Who else touches it

Stripe processes payments. Their privacy policy governs the card data they hold. We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not share it except with that processor and where we are legally required.

How long

Order records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Delivery correspondence is kept while it is useful for resends and disputes. Marketing consent, if you gave it, lasts until you withdraw it.

Your choices

Write to support@xevolution.biz to ask for a copy of what we hold, to correct it, or to have it deleted where the law allows. Unsubscribe links in any marketing mail work immediately.

Cookies

This page and the checkout do not set advertising cookies of ours. Stripe sets what it needs to process a payment securely and to prevent fraud.
