Before you pay anything

The questions people actually ask.

Including the ones a sales page usually avoids: what this is not, what the guarantee really covers, and what happens the day after you buy.

Is this a course?

Partly. It is 112 working documents: SOPs, templates, scorecards and models you open and fill in, plus four written workbooks — one per paid level, 24 lessons in total. There is no video curriculum and nothing to watch.

Who is this actually for?

Owners of cold-email agencies with one to five people, running roughly two to fifteen retainers at $2,000 to $5,000 a month. You already send. The problem is not knowing how to send; it is that nothing is written down, deliverability is handled by memory, and reporting is improvised the day before the client calls.

It is not for someone who has never sent a cold email. There is no step-by-step teaching of the business here.

What does it cost, and how is the price structured?

There are four levels, from $29 to $497. Each level is a one-time payment, not a subscription. The full comparison is on the levels page.

Each level ships a defined slice, and the slices stack. Level 1 is 17 documents, Level 2 is 45 in total, Level 3 is 62 in total, and Level 4 is the complete 112. Only the $497 level carries the whole inventory on the inventory page; the three below it carry their own slice plus everything under them.

How do I get the files after I pay?

Immediately, as a download. There is no drip schedule, no module that unlocks in week three, and no waiting for content to be recorded. Every file listed in your level is available the moment the payment clears.

Is there a guarantee?

There is a 30-day refund window, and the exact wording is in section 4 of the terms of service. Read it before you buy. It covers the standard case: you bought, you opened it, and it was not what this page described.

It does not cover results. Nothing here guarantees a client, a meeting or a retainer, and the terms say so in plain language.

Will the scorecards actually calculate, or are they pictures of spreadsheets?

They calculate. The 16 Excel workbooks carry live formulas. The Domain Health Scorecard, for example, takes five weighted metrics, applies thresholds in the sheet, and outputs a band and an action in the same row. You put the numbers in; the sheet gives you the answer.

Can I put my own logo on the client-facing documents?

Yes. The 11 Word templates are editable, and the licence permits you to brand them as your own for use with your clients. You cannot resell the files themselves as a product. The licence page states exactly where that line sits.

Do I need any particular software?

A PDF reader, a spreadsheet application that opens .xlsx files (Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice), and a word processor that opens .docx files. Nothing else. No account, no login, no proprietary platform.

How many people can use it?

The licence covers one agency, with no limit on how many people inside that agency open the files. It is not per-person. The licence page sets out what is and is not allowed.

Is this a subscription or a one-time purchase?

One-time. You buy once and the files are yours to keep. Future revisions are delivered to buyers at no additional charge, and the delivery date for those revisions is stated on the inventory page.

What is the difference between the free audit and the paid product?

The free 20-Minute Deliverability Audit is one document: a short checklist you run against a single domain. The paid product is the full system the audit belongs to, with the scorecards, reporting engine, sequences, QA gates and runbook that keep it running across clients and months.

What happens when I get stuck or something is wrong?

Write to support@xevolution.biz and a person reads it. There is no ticket queue hidden behind a chatbot. Support covers access, files and licensing. It does not include consulting on your agency.

Can I see a sample before I buy?

Yes. The free 20-Minute Deliverability Audit is a real document from the same folder the paid files come from. Run it on one of your domains first. That is the honest way to judge whether the rest is written the way you work.

Why is the top level priced where it is?

Because it is not a file bundle at that point. The upper levels carry the full inventory plus the retention reporting engine, the 90-day runbook and the team layer. The exact contents of every level are listed on the levels page so you can compare before deciding.

What do I do in the first week?

Open the Startup Guide. It gives you an order of operations rather than a table of contents. The short version: run the free audit on your own domain, put the Domain Health Scorecard in place for your biggest client, then build the first monthly report from the Retention Reporting Engine. That is the first week, not the first month.

Still deciding

The inventory page lists every file by name. If that list is not what you needed, this is not the product for you, and that is worth knowing before you pay.
