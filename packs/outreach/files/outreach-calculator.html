Cold Email Outreach Calculator

Enter your own numbers. The calculator turns them into deliverable contacts, replies, meetings, deals and revenue. It changes nothing until you type in it, and it stores nothing.

Your inputs

What your numbers produce

Deliverable contacts 0

How many of your monthly leads actually reach an inbox after deliverability is applied. This is the real size of your audience.

Positive replies 0

How many people reply in a way that shows interest. This is what most cold email reporting misses by counting all replies together.

Meetings booked 0

How many calls land on your calendar once positive replies are converted into booked meetings.

Closed deals 0

How many meetings turn into paying clients at your stated close rate. This is the number that pays you.

Monthly revenue $0

Closed deals multiplied by average deal size. This is new contract value added per month, not cash collected this month.

Cost per meeting n/a

Your monthly sending cost divided by meetings booked. It tells you what one conversation costs you to buy.

Revenue per meeting n/a

Monthly revenue divided by meetings booked. Compare it against cost per meeting to see whether a meeting is worth buying at all.

Assumptions, read this before you trust a number

These outputs are arithmetic on your own inputs. They are not a forecast and they are not a promise of income.

Defaults loaded on this page:

  • Leads per month: 1000
  • Deliverability rate: 95%
  • Positive reply rate: 5%
  • Meeting booked rate from positive replies: 30%
  • Close rate on meetings: 20%
  • Average deal size: $2,000
  • Current monthly sending cost: $300

Every one of those defaults is an assumption, not a measurement of your business. Real reply rates move with your list, your offer, your domain reputation and the month. Change the inputs to your own observed numbers. If you have no observed numbers yet, treat the result as a guess and test for one month before you plan around it.
