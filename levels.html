The upgrade credit

If you buy a level and decide within 60 days that you want the next one, the price you already paid comes off it. A $29 Level 1 buyer upgrading to Level 2 pays $97 − $29 = $68.

How it works in practice: email the support address at the bottom of this page from the address you paid with, quote your receipt number, and we send you a payment link for the credited amount. There is no account system yet, so a human does this by hand, normally the same business day. It is not automatic at checkout, and this page does not claim it is.

The same terms are in section 4B of the terms. If the two ever disagree, the terms govern.

Refunds, in plain language

Thirty days from purchase to request a refund. If the reason is that a tool did not do what it says it does, we ask for the completed worksheet from that tool — that is the whole test.

The money goes back the same business day it is approved. You keep full access for five days from approval; after that the file set is withdrawn. The Domain Health Scorecard, the Startup Guide and the licence stay yours permanently.

Because these are files you download, that withdrawal runs on your honour until there is an account system. The terms say so in writing.
